About

Technical depth, without disciplinary walls.

My background combines computer science engineering, health informatics, immersive technology, and human–computer interaction. I completed my PhD at the University of Pannonia in 2025, researching VR and game-based approaches to cognitive development and rehabilitation.

I do not see these fields as boundaries. They are a foundation for investigating new questions—including human-centred AI—and for contributing wherever rigorous research, thoughtful engineering, and interdisciplinary collaboration can create value. My industry experience also shapes how I approach prototypes: as robust, understandable systems that should be useful beyond a demo.
