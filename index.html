About

Technical depth, shaped by interdisciplinary work.

My background combines computer science engineering, health informatics, immersive technology, and human–computer interaction. I completed my PhD at the University of Pannonia in 2025, researching VR and game-based approaches to cognitive development and rehabilitation.

Before and alongside research, I worked in application and IT engineering roles involving software development, testing, containers, CI/CD, Linux systems, and product coordination. That experience informs how I approach research prototypes: as systems that must be robust, understandable, and useful beyond a demo.
